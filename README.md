## Prototype Flow

- Practice card -> Practice detail destination
- Patient card -> Patient detail destination
- Financial card -> Financial detail destination
- Clinical card -> Clinical detail destination

The four launch-path cards on the Executive Snapshot frame are wired as prototype interactions to their matching section detail screens. The Overview/back controls on the destination screens navigate back to the Executive Snapshot.
